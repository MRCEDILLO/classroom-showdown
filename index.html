The easiest way to start is to download the sample set and edit it in Excel or Google Sheets, then save it as CSV. The first row holds the column names. Every question needs at least options A and B, and the answer is the letter of the right option. Explanation and topics are optional. Without a topics column, questions are put into columns of five in file order. The board holds up to 6 topics with 5 questions each.
